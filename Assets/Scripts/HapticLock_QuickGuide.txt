WHAT YOU FEEL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

When drill hits 90°:
    👆 Immediate HARD STOP on finger
    👆 Cannot bend finger at all
    👆 Can't pull trigger!
    👆 Feels like trigger is frozen/jammed

While locked:
    👆 Constant wall preventing movement
    👆 Feels completely "frozen"
    👆 Strong haptic warning

When unlocked at 50°:
    👆 Sudden release!
    👆 Finger can bend again
    👆 Trigger responds normally

QUICK SETTINGS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

In DrillHeatSystem Inspector:

    [Haptic Feedback]
    ☑ Enable Haptic Lock        ← Turn on/off
    Normal End Flexion: 0.5      ← Normal limit
    Locked End Flexion: 0.3      ← Lock limit (frozen!)


Turn OFF:
    ☐ Enable Haptic Lock

Slightly less restrictive (tiny movement allowed):
    Locked End Flexion: 0.35

Maximum lock (absolutely frozen):
    Locked End Flexion: 0.0
