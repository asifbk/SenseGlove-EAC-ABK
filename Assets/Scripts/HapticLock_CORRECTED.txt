═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHY THIS WORKS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SenseGlove trigger logic works like this:

    latestPressure = Mathf.InverseLerp(startFlexion, endFlexion, currFlex);
    
    InverseLerp maps a value between two bounds to 0-1.

NORMAL OPERATION:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    startFlexion: 0.3
    endFlexion:   0.5
    Range:        0.2 (can bend from 0.3 to 0.5)
    
    When your finger is at:
        0.3 → pressure = 0 (trigger not pulled)
        0.4 → pressure = 0.5 (half pulled)
        0.5 → pressure = 1 (fully pulled)
        0.6+ → still pressure = 1 (clamped at max)
    
    ✓ You can bend your finger from 0.3 to 0.5


WRONG APPROACH (endFlexion = 1.0):
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    startFlexion: 0.3
    endFlexion:   1.0  ← WRONG!
    Range:        0.7 (can bend from 0.3 to 1.0!)
    
    When your finger is at:
        0.3 → pressure = 0
        0.5 → pressure = 0.28
        0.7 → pressure = 0.57
        1.0 → pressure = 1
    
    ✗ You can bend EVEN MORE (up to 1.0)!
    ✗ This is the OPPOSITE of locking!


CORRECT APPROACH (endFlexion = 0.3):
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    startFlexion: 0.3
    endFlexion:   0.3  ← CORRECT!
    Range:        0.0 (ZERO range = locked!)
    
    When your finger is at:
        0.3 → pressure = 0/0 = undefined (no range)
        0.4 → pressure = clamped to 1
        0.5 → pressure = clamped to 1
    
    ✓ NO range means NO movement allowed!
    ✓ Finger is FROZEN at 0.3
    ✓ Cannot bend beyond start position
    ✓ Creates a hard stop/wall
