DRILL HEAT SYSTEM - SIMPLE SETUP INSTRUCTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Follow these 6 simple steps:

STEP 1: Select the Drill
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
In the Hierarchy panel, click on:
    DummyDrill-Rigged

You should see it highlighted and its properties appear in the Inspector.


STEP 2: Add DrillParticleSetup Component
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
At the bottom of the Inspector panel:
    
    1. Click the [Add Component] button
    2. Type "DrillParticleSetup" in the search box
    3. Click on "DrillParticleSetup" when it appears


STEP 3: Assign the Drill Bit Reference
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
In the Inspector, you'll now see the DrillParticleSetup component.

Find the field that says: "Drill Bit Tip"

METHOD A - Drag and Drop:
    1. Look at your Hierarchy panel
    2. Expand: DummyDrill-Rigged → Model → RotatingHead → Drill holder
    3. Click and DRAG "Drillbit" 
    4. DROP it onto the "Drill Bit Tip" field in the Inspector

METHOD B - Use the Picker:
    1. Click the small circle icon (⊙) next to "Drill Bit Tip"
    2. A window will pop up
    3. Type "Drillbit" in the search
    4. Double-click "Drillbit" from the results


STEP 4: Click the Big Green Button
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
In the Inspector, you'll see a BIG GREEN BUTTON that says:

    ╔═══════════════════════════════════════╗
    ║  SETUP PARTICLE SYSTEMS               ║
    ╚═══════════════════════════════════════╝

Click this button!

A dialog will appear saying "Success!" - Click "Great!"

The particle systems have now been created!


STEP 5: Add DrillHeatSystem Component
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
With DummyDrill-Rigged still selected:

    1. Click [Add Component] again
    2. Type "DrillHeatSystem"
    3. Click on "DrillHeatSystem" when it appears

The component will automatically find the particles you just created!


STEP 6: Clean Up (Remove DrillParticleSetup)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
In the Inspector, find the DrillParticleSetup component header.

On the right side of the component name, click the three dots (⋮) or gear icon (⚙)

Select "Remove Component"

Click "Yes" to confirm.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
DONE! 🎉
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Press the PLAY button to test it!

Grab the drill and start drilling into the wooden table.

You should see:
    ✓ Burning smell particles at 50% heat
    ✓ Smoke particles at 70% heat
    ✓ Drill bit glowing red when hot

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
TROUBLESHOOTING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: I don't see the big green button!
A: Make sure you've added the DrillParticleSetup component and the 
   Inspector is showing. Try clicking "DummyDrill-Rigged" again.

Q: The button says "Missing Reference"!
A: You need to assign the "Drill Bit Tip" field first (Step 3).

Q: I can't find "Drillbit" in the Hierarchy!
A: Expand the folders: DummyDrill-Rigged → Model → RotatingHead → 
   Drill holder → You should see "Drillbit" there.

Q: No effects appear when I press Play!
A: Make sure you completed ALL 6 steps, especially adding DrillHeatSystem
   in Step 5. Also check that WoodDustSystem GameObject is enabled.
